# IRDAI text samples

These page-aware extracts come from official IRDAI documents listed in
`data/manifest.csv`. They are small fixtures for developing and testing extraction,
chunking, retrieval, and citations. They are not a substitute for the complete source
documents.

- `master-circular-health-insurance-2024-pages-8-10.txt`: cashless authorization,
  discharge, claim repudiation, multiple policies, and grievances.
- `master-circular-policyholder-interests-2024-pages-30-34.txt`: claim processing,
  cashless and reimbursement timelines, multiple policies, grievances, and Ombudsman
  escalation.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India. IRDAI's website
policy permits accurate reproduction with prominent acknowledgement, except for
third-party copyrighted material. Always preserve the source document, PDF page, and
official URL when using an extract.
